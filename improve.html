Suggestions for the PK Hosting team

How pkhosting.com could be better

I checked pkhosting.com live on 11 November 2026 and found four things worth fixing, each with the exact fix. Nothing on pkhosting.com itself was changed. These are suggestions, for the team that runs the site.

Four things to fix

1. The old client area address returns a 404

What I checked: pkhosting.com/clientarea.php returns a 404 page. That was the old WHMCS client area address, and old links, bookmarks and search results still point at it.

Exact fix: add a 301 redirect from /clientarea.php to /login/, which is the live sign-in page. One redirect rule, no page to build.

2. The featured blog article is written for cPanel, but PK Hosting runs Hestia

What I checked: the featured article on the blog is "508 Resource Limit Is Reached: How to Fix It in cPanel". It tells the reader to check cPanel menus. PK Hosting runs the Hestia panel (panel.pkhosting.com), not cPanel, so a customer following the article will not find those menus.

Exact fix: rewrite the article for the Hestia dashboard: where to see resource usage in Hestia, what the limits mean there, and what to send support. Keep the title honest by dropping "in cPanel".

3. No Trustpilot profile shows up in search

What I checked: a search for PK Hosting on Trustpilot on 11 November 2026 returned other Pakistani hosts (Pakish, Cheaphosting.pk, Hoster.pk, WebHoster.pk) but no profile for pkhosting.com.

Exact fix: create and claim a Trustpilot business profile for pkhosting.com, then invite real customers to review after their service goes live. Never buy reviews and never write them.

4. No Google Business Profile shows up in search

What I checked: a search for a PK Hosting Google Business Profile on 11 November 2026 returned other Lahore hosting companies with profiles, but none for PK Hosting.

Exact fix: create a Google Business Profile for the Lahore office at 231-B Main Boulevard, Phase 8 DHA, Lahore, with the phone (042) 35692952 and the website pkhosting.com, then verify it. It is free, and it is where local customers look first.
